oMcParty by omentejovem

My first hundred series. Burn a Mc Moon edition to claim one of the eighty-five. Fifteen Golden Moons stay with the artist and head to auction.

what's a golden moon?Golden Moons are Mc Moon editions I've burned to participate on the series with you, they were bought back through years on the secondary market since the Mc Moon launch on December 28, 2022. I burn all 15 Mc Moons myself before the release, and the 15 works they unlock go to auction marked as Golden Moons on November 11–12, alongside every work not allocated through burns. In them lives the concept documenting the inevitable bear market, across crypto and the art market. The only difference between a Golden Moon and the rest is in the metadata, marking my personal choice.

Questions and answers

questions from the counter
What is oMcParty?

A series I had so much fun making. It starts from the McDonald's parties I loved as a kid, and mixes all that nostalgia with how I see the world today, crypto culture and everything in between.

I've always told my collectors not to expect utility from my work, and this isn't utility either. It's a new chapter: almost four years after Mc Moon, my first 1/1/100 is born.

My most sincere thanks to everyone who held Mc Moon for the art and is joining my party today. The auction day, November 11, is my birthday. I'm turning 25, with grit, optimism and a real passion for what I do, and I couldn't ask for a better celebration than being here with you and my practice. I hope to see you in 5, 10, 20, 35 more years to come.

Let's make CryptoArt fun again, together.

How do I get an artwork?
Burn a Mc Moon edition during the burn window, November 1–8. Every completed burn guarantees one artwork. Burning and minting are separate actions: you burn first, and your artwork is allocated after the reveal.
What happens to my Mc Moon when I burn it?
It's gone for good: the edition is destroyed on-chain and can't be recovered. In exchange, every completed burn guarantees you one oMcParty artwork.
Should I pick or let the kitchen surprise me?
Both start with a burn. Pick costs 0.20 ETH extra and lets you choose your artwork in the feed during the 48-hour selection window that opens with the November 9 reveal. Surprise is free: you receive a random artwork on November 11.
I paid to pick but didn't choose in time. What happens?
You still get an artwork. Anyone who paid 0.20 ETH and hasn't chosen when the 48-hour window closes receives a randomly assigned one.
Can I burn after November 8?
No. Every burn has to be completed before the November 9 reveal. There are no additional burns during either allocation phase, so the pool stays fixed once the menu is revealed.
Can I burn more than one Mc Moon?
Yes. One completed burn equals one artwork, so two burns guarantee two artworks.
How does the random allocation work?
Random artworks are drawn from every piece still available after the selection window closes, so everyone in the surprise pool has the same odds. You'll see which artwork is yours once the auctions close on November 12.
Why is the feed shuffled?
On purpose. The works appear in random order, mixed across every trait, so you discover the series one surprise at a time. The full menu, sorted by trait, opens once your artwork is allocated.
What is a Golden Moon?
One of the 15 Mc Moon editions I bought back myself on the secondary market since the Mc Moon launch on December 28, 2022. I burn all 15 before the release, and the 15 artworks they unlock are the ones I auction. The only difference between a Golden Moon and any other work is in the metadata, marking my personal choice.
What happens to works nobody claims?
Any artwork not allocated through burns joins the 15 Golden Moons at auction.
How do the auctions work?
They run for 24 hours on November 11–12, starting at 0 ETH. A bid in the final 10 minutes extends the auction by another 10 minutes, so nobody wins by sniping at the last second.
What is the Companion?
A signed, year-dated print of your artwork on Hahnemühle Photo Rag 310 gsm, with a passe-partout and frame in white, black, red, yellow or blue, a certificate of authenticity and a custom stainless-steel holder, shipped in a wooden crate with protective foam. $333 + shipping. Each one is made to order: production can take 2–4 weeks before it ships. For interactive works, the print uses the static thumbnail, or another final static version you choose.
When can I claim my Companion, and how do I pay?
Claims open November 12, once the auctions end and every artwork is allocated. You can pay by credit card or crypto.
How will people know a print was claimed?
Your NFT's metadata updates as soon as the print is claimed and paid for, not when it ships, and it clearly shows whether a print has been claimed.
What are tips in Big Macs?
After each claim you can tip the artist in Big Macs, sent as ETH. It's optional, and how much is entirely up to you. The ten biggest tippers each receive a unique surprise limited edition, minted on my official editions contract, reveal to happen after the auctions.
